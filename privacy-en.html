Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Beyond IPTV is a media player for Android phones, tablets and TVs that plays IPTV playlists the user adds themselves (an M3U URL, an M3U file or an Xtream Codes account). This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

In short: No account is needed. No personal data is sent to the developer, and the app contains no ads, analytics or tracking tools. Everything stays on your device.

1. The app does not provide content

Beyond IPTV does not provide, sell or recommend any streams, channels, movies or series. All content comes from sources the user adds; the user is responsible for using those sources lawfully.

2. Information stored on your device

  • Playlist details: the M3U URL, Xtream server address, username and password you enter. Addresses and passwords are encrypted with the Android Keystore.
  • Playlist content: channel, movie and series names, categories, logos and TV guide (EPG) data, kept in an on-device database for fast browsing.
  • Usage data: favorites, watch history and resume positions, recent searches, settings, hidden or locked categories.
  • Parental lock PIN: stored only as an irreversible hash (PBKDF2).

This information never leaves your device and is excluded from Android backups.

3. Servers the app connects to

The app only connects to sources you add: playlist and stream servers, TV guide (XMLTV) addresses, and the logo / poster images those sources reference. These are third parties of your choosing; technical information such as your IP address may be processed by them under their own policies.

4. Adding a playlist to a TV with your phone (local network)

When adding a playlist on a TV you can optionally use your phone via a QR code. The TV then opens a temporary page that is reachable only from the same local network and only with a one-time code. What you enter goes directly from your phone to your TV, not over the internet, and the page closes when you are done.

5. Voice search

When you press the microphone button on a TV, the device's own speech recognition service (for example Google's) is used. Microphone permission is requested only when you press that button. The app does not store audio or send it to the developer; it only receives the recognized text. Audio processing is subject to that service's privacy policy.

6. Notifications and the TV home screen

Program reminders are scheduled on the device and shown as notifications. On Android TV / Google TV, items you are watching and your favorite channels are written to the system's on-device TV provider for the "Play Next" row and the Beyond IPTV row.

7. Deleting your data

In Settings you can clear favorites, watch history and recent searches, and delete playlists. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from the device.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children. Because content comes from the user's own sources, categories can be locked with a PIN or hidden using the parental lock.

9. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, this page will be updated. Questions: github.com/semdin/beyond-iptv-site/issues
